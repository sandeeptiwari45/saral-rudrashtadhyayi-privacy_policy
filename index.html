Privacy Policy

Application: Saral Rudrashtadhyayi (सरल रुद्राष्टाध्यायी)

Last Updated: August 2026

Summary: The Saral Rudrashtadhyayi app is an offline, religious recitation and reading application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage files.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or sign in to use this app.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not track device unique IDs (IMEI, MAC address, etc.) or collect personal telemetry.

2. Local Data Storage

To provide a better reading experience, the app stores certain user preferences locally on your device only:

  • Bookmarks: Mantras or chapters you choose to save for quick access.
  • Display Preferences: Font size, theme mode (Light/Dark/Saffron), screen wake-lock settings.

This data is stored purely within your device's local storage (via standard shared preferences) and is never transmitted to any external server or third party.

3. Permissions

The application only uses minimal, non-sensitive permissions necessary for basic app functionality:

  • WAKE_LOCK: Used optionally to keep the device screen on while you are reciting mantras, preventing the screen from turning off mid-prayer.

4. Third-Party Services

The app is completely offline and does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or profiling SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for all age groups, including children (All Ages 3+). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children or any other users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
